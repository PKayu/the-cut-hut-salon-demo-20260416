Our Story

Ogden's Neighborhood Salon

The Cut Hut has been a trusted name on Harrison Blvd, serving families across Ogden and the surrounding communities. We started with a simple idea: everyone deserves a great haircut and a warm, welcoming experience — without paying salon prices that feel out of reach.

Over the years, we've built a loyal community of clients who trust us with everything from their kids' first haircuts to special-occasion color and styling. We take real pride in what we do, and we take real care of the people who come through our door.

Our stylists are skilled in cuts, perms, color, weaves, waxing, and deep conditioning — and they genuinely listen to what you want before they pick up their scissors.
