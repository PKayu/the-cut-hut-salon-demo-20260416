Our Story

Northern Utah's Favorite Family Salon

The Cut Hut has been a go-to destination on Harrison Blvd for families across Ogden and the surrounding area. We believe everyone deserves a great haircut — and a great experience — without breaking the bank.

Our team of experienced stylists specializes in everything from taper fades and perms for teenagers, to color treatments and formal styles for special occasions. We welcome walk-ins and gladly call ahead for color appointments.
